# 02_Era_VPN_KPI_Tracking_System.md - Business Health Monitor

## 📊 KPI Overview
Monitor Era VPN business health, make data-driven decisions, and identify growth opportunities early.

## 💰 Financial Pillars
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue):** Target 60,000 MMK (Month 1) -> 200,000 MMK (Month 3).
- **Daily Revenue:** Track at 9 PM daily. Alert if 3 consecutive days of decline.
- **Gross Margin:** Maintain ≥40%. Alert if <35%.
- **Cash Flow:** Minimum buffer 20,000 MMK. Alert if <25,000 MMK.

## 👥 Customer Metrics
- **CAC (Acquisition Cost):** Keep <500 MMK per customer.
- **Retention:** Target 75% by Month 3. Alert if <50%.
- **CSAT (Satisfaction):** Target ≥4.2/5 stars. Alert if any customer rates ≤2.
- **Referral Rate:** Target 30% by Month 3.

## ⚡ Operational Efficiency
- **Order Fulfillment:** Target <2 hours. Alert if >3 hours.
- **Support Response:** Target <1 hour. Alert if >2 hours.
- **Key Replacement:** Acceptable <5%. Critical >10% (Switch supplier).

## 📅 Reporting Cadence (David & Ko Tun Task)
- **Daily Dashboard:** inquiries, orders, revenue, pending deliveries, support status.
- **Weekly Report:** Acquisition trends, weekly revenue, conversion rates.
- **Monthly Post-mortem:** Comprehensive P&L, cohort analysis, strategic adjustments.
