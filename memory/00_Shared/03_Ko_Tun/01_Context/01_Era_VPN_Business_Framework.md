2. **Automate ruthlessly:** AI handles 80% of operations.
3. **Customer-first always:** 1-hour response guarantee.
4. **Build systems, not dependencies:** Document everything.
5. **Test fast, iterate faster:** Launch in 3 days, improve daily.
